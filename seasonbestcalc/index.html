🏆 Season Bests Export

Upload your current season-bests CSV, paste in a new meet's results, and get back every athlete who either set a new season best or ran/threw an event for the first time this year.

1. Upload current season bests (optional, but recommended)

Export this from your PWA at yoursite/#seasonbests → pick the current season → "By Athlete" → download CSV. Re-upload any time you want to refresh the baseline (e.g. after entering last week's marks).

2. Add a meet's results

Useful for the first meet of the season when nobody's in the database yet, or whenever you just want a full roster of times regardless of whether it beat anything.
